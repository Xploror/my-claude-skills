# Reference cover letter — tone & structure template

This is a fully anonymized template showing the target register: confident, flowing, comma-chained clauses, contrast framing in the expertise paragraph. Every bracketed placeholder must be filled with THIS applicant's real details — never carry over a previous applicant's name, background, or achievements into a new one. When a new cover letter doesn't sound like the pattern below, revise it rather than deliver it.

## What to notice (apply regardless of the applicant's field)

- Paragraph 1 (intro) is one long sentence chaining "what the role is" → "why it matters" → "why it's exactly my path" — not three short sentences.
- Paragraph 2 (expertise) moves organization-by-organization through real experience, each sentence ending on the specific JD-relevant skill it proves. Never invent an organization, achievement, or metric that isn't in the applicant's real source material.
- Paragraph 3 (application) opens with a contrast claim ("What sets me apart...") before restating evidence — this is what turns a list of facts into a pitch.
- Paragraph 4 (closing) is short and doesn't repeat earlier content — just invites the conversation.
- Commas do a lot of work here instead of periods — clauses build on each other rather than resetting with each sentence.
- This pattern works for any field — swap in the applicant's actual industry, tools, and achievements; don't force aerospace/software-specific language onto an unrelated applicant.
